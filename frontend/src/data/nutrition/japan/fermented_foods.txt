FERMENTED FOODS FOR JAPANESE RUNNERS

NATTO (FERMENTED SOYBEANS): World richest source of vitamin K2 (menaquinone-7). K2 directs calcium to bones. Protein: 17g per 100g, complete protein. Nattokinase enzyme improves blood flow and oxygen delivery. Bacillus subtilis probiotic survives stomach acid unlike most probiotics. Traditional protocol: 1 pack daily with rice, mustard, soy sauce. Reduces DOMS, improves gut microbiome diversity linked to endurance.

MISO (FERMENTED SOYBEAN PASTE): Lactobacillus probiotic for gut health and immune function. Sodium for electrolyte replacement post-run. 1 cup miso soup post-run is a Japanese runner staple. White miso mild for post-run, red miso stronger for training days.

FISH COLLAGEN PEPTIDES (GYOYO COLLAGEN): Derived from fish scales and skin. More bioavailable than bovine collagen — smaller peptide size. Japan has highest per-capita fish collagen consumption globally. Standard protocol: 5-10g fish collagen + vitamin C (yuzu or supplement), daily morning. For runners: take 30 min before morning run for tendon and cartilage support.

UMEBOSHI (PICKLED PLUM): Citric acid reduces lactic acid accumulation during runs. Traditional Japanese sports recovery food for centuries. Contains natural sodium and minerals. 1-2 umeboshi daily, or in water during long training runs.

AMAZAKE (SWEET FERMENTED RICE): Natural maltose for rapid carb replenishment post-run. Rich in B vitamins from fermentation, particularly B1 for energy metabolism. Koji enzymes improve nutrient absorption. Traditional post-ekiden relay drink.

MICROBIOME AND RUNNING: Japanese diet produces highly diverse gut microbiome from lifelong fermented food consumption. Research 2022: athletes with high Veillonellaceae bacteria have better marathon times. This bacteria converts lactate to propionate — fuel for muscle cells. Japanese runners show naturally higher diversity linked to natto and miso.

GUT HEALTH PROTOCOL: Natto daily + miso soup twice daily + tsukemono with meals. This combination achieves diverse probiotic intake without supplements.
