RUNNER NUTRITION GUIDE — JAPAN

JAPANESE FOODS FOR RUNNERS: Onigiri (rice ball) — perfect pre/mid-run carb, 35-40g carbs per ball, portable. Miso soup — sodium, potassium, probiotics post-run. Edamame — complete protein 11g per 100g, leucine-rich for muscle synthesis. Natto — nattokinase improves blood flow, vitamin K2, 15g protein per 100g. Soba (buckwheat) — 6g protein per 100g, rutin antioxidant. Salmon (sake) — 2g EPA+DHA per 100g, highest omega-3 fish. Mackerel (saba) — budget omega-3, grilled mackerel is classic athlete food. Umeboshi — citric acid reduces lactic acid. 1-2 per day.

PRE-RUN JAPANESE PROTOCOL: 2-3 hours before: rice + miso soup + small fish + pickled vegetables. 30-60 min before: 1-2 onigiri + green tea or sports drink. Race day: mochi (rice cakes) for carb loading — high GI, easy to eat large amounts.

POST-RUN RECOVERY: Grilled salmon + rice + miso soup — complete recovery meal. 2 onsen tamago (soft eggs) + 200ml soy milk + edamame — 20g protein. Natto + rice + pickles — fermented protein + carbs. Amazake post-race — rapid carb replenishment, B vitamins, enzymes.

JAPANESE PRECISION NUTRITION: Target 1.6-2.0g protein per kg bodyweight. Take protein within 30 min post-run: 20-30g. Carbohydrate periodisation: more carbs on hard training days, less on recovery days.

HYDRATION JAPANESE METHOD: Drink before thirst — thirst means already 1-2% dehydrated. Pocari Sweat during runs — clinically developed Na/K ratio. Green tea before short runs: caffeine + catechins, avoid for runs over 60 min. Traditional: umeboshi in water — sodium, citric acid, minerals.

BUDGET RECOVERY MEAL JAPAN: Salmon onigiri (150 yen) + miso soup (50 yen) + edamame (100 yen) + green tea = complete recovery under 400 yen.
