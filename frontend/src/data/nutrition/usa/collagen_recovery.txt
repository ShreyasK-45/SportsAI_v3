COLLAGEN AND RECOVERY NUTRITION — USA RUNNERS

COLLAGEN PEPTIDES PROTOCOL: 15g hydrolysed collagen + 500mg vitamin C, taken 30-60 minutes before training. Timing matters — vitamin C is the required cofactor for collagen synthesis in tendons. Clinical trials show 12-week protocol improves tendon stiffness and reduces injury recurrence in distance runners.

PROTEIN TIMING: Consume 25-40g high quality protein within 45 minutes post-run. Whey isolate absorbs fastest. Whole food alternatives: 5oz chicken breast (42g), 5oz tuna (35g), 1 cup cottage cheese (28g), 3 large eggs plus 1 cup Greek yoghurt (35g combined).

SLEEP NUTRITION: Casein protein (cottage cheese 1 cup = 28g) before sleep. Slow-digesting — releases amino acids across 7-8 hours of overnight muscle repair. Magnesium glycinate 300-400mg aids sleep quality and recovery.

ANTIOXIDANT RECOVERY STACK: Tart cherry 480ml + blueberries 1 cup daily. Reduces CRP (C-reactive protein) and IL-6 inflammatory markers. Most effective 3-5 days after a long race or hard workout.

HYDRATION PRECISION: Sweat test method — weigh before and after 1-hour run. Each 0.5kg lost = 500ml fluid deficit. Replace with electrolyte drink containing 500-700mg sodium per litre. LMNT, Nuun Sport, or home mix: 500ml water + 1/4 tsp salt + squeeze lemon.

CARBOHYDRATE LOADING: 2 days before key race or long run: 10-12g carbs per kg bodyweight. Example for 70kg runner: 700-840g carbs daily. Foods: pasta, rice, bagels, bananas, sweet potato, sports drinks. Reduce fibre and fat — avoid beans, cruciferous vegetables night before race.

BEETROOT / NITRATE PROTOCOL: 300-500ml beet juice or 400mg nitrate supplement. Take 2-3 hours before long run or race. Dilates blood vessels, reduces oxygen cost of exercise 3-5%. Combine with collagen protocol on long training days for maximum benefit.

BUDGET RECOVERY USA: 5oz canned tuna + 1 cup brown rice + frozen spinach + tablespoon olive oil = 40g protein, 65g carbs, under $3.50. Add lemon for vitamin C to absorb non-heme iron from spinach.
