ANTI-INFLAMMATORY NUTRITION — USA

TART CHERRY JUICE: Most studied anti-inflammatory food for runners. 480ml daily reduces DOMS by 30% and accelerates strength recovery. Anthocyanins inhibit COX-1 and COX-2 enzymes — same pathway as ibuprofen without GI risk. Drink 3-5 days post long run or race.

OMEGA-3 FATTY ACIDS: EPA and DHA reduce systemic inflammation. Target 2-3g daily. Best sources: wild salmon, sardines, mackerel, herring. Supplement: fish oil capsules or algae oil for vegans. Reduces IL-6 and TNF-alpha inflammatory cytokines elevated post long run.

COLLAGEN PEPTIDES PROTOCOL: 15g collagen peptides + 500mg vitamin C, 30-60 min before morning run or exercise. Timing matters — vitamin C cofactor enables collagen synthesis in tendons during the exercise stimulus. Clinical evidence for 12-week protocol.

CREATINE MONOHYDRATE: 3-5g daily. Improves sprint performance and recovery capacity. Most studied sports supplement. No loading phase needed. Take any time — consistency matters more than timing.

CAFFEINE FOR PERFORMANCE: 3-6mg per kg bodyweight, 30-60 min pre-run. Improves endurance performance 3-7%. Coffee works as well as caffeine supplements. Avoid in last 6 hours before sleep.

BLUEBERRIES: 1 cup daily. Highest antioxidant content of any common fruit. Polyphenols reduce oxidative stress markers 20% post exercise. Frozen identical to fresh — buy in bulk.

TURMERIC GOLDEN MILK: 1 tsp turmeric + pinch black pepper + almond milk + honey. Curcumin anti-inflammatory. Must combine with piperine from black pepper for 2000% better absorption.

MAGNESIUM GLYCINATE: 300-400mg before sleep. Reduces cramps, improves sleep quality, supports protein synthesis overnight. Most bioavailable magnesium form.
