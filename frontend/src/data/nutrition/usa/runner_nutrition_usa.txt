RUNNER NUTRITION GUIDE — USA

PRE-RUN (1-2 hours before): Oatmeal + banana + almond butter — slow-release carbs, healthy fat, classic. Whole grain toast + peanut butter + honey — quick and practical. Greek yoghurt + granola + berries — protein + carbs + antioxidants. Bagel + cream cheese — high-GI carb load for long runs or race day.

POST-RUN RECOVERY (target 25-40g protein within 30-45 min): Chocolate milk — clinically proven optimal: 15g protein + 26g carbs, ideal 3:1 carb-to-protein ratio. Whey protein isolate — 25-30g per scoop, fastest absorbing. Chicken breast 6oz — 42g protein, complete amino acids. Greek yoghurt 1 cup — 17-20g protein with probiotics. Tuna 5oz can — 35g protein, omega-3 rich, budget-friendly.

CARBOHYDRATE STRATEGY: Post-run within 30 min: chocolate milk or sports drink. Long run >90 min: 30-60g carbs per hour during run — GU gel, Clif bloks, Tailwind. Carb loading 2 days pre-race: 10-12g per kg bodyweight. Pasta, rice, bread, potatoes.

HYDRATION USA: 500ml water 2 hours before run. 150-250ml every 15-20 min during run. Electrolyte drinks: Nuun, LMNT, Liquid IV — target sodium 500-700mg per litre. Warning: hyponatremia risk from excessive plain water in marathons.

PLANT-BASED USA: B12 mandatory for strict vegans 1000mcg daily. Key proteins: Impossible Burger 19g, Beyond Meat 20g, tempeh 19g per 100g, edamame 11g per 100g. Pea + rice protein blend matches whey in muscle synthesis studies.

BUDGET RECOVERY MEAL: 5oz tuna + brown rice + frozen broccoli + olive oil — 40g protein, 60g carbs, under $4.
